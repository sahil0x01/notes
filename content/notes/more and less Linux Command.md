The [`more`](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/aix/7.3?topic=m-more-command) and [`less`](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man1/less.1.html) commands let you view the contents of a file, one page (or line) at a time [in](https://www.boot.dev/lessons/8d06ba1c-108f-4f01-9e2d-826706754763#:~:text=The%20more%20and%20less%20commands%20let%20you%20view%20the%20contents%20of%20a%20file%2C%20one%20page%20%28or%20line%29%20at%20a%20time) [[Learn Linux]]

The `less` command does everything that the `more` command does but also has more features. As a general rule, you should use `less` instead of `more` [in](https://www.boot.dev/lessons/8d06ba1c-108f-4f01-9e2d-826706754763#:~:text=The%20less%20command%20does%20everything%20that%20the%20more%20command%20does%20but%20also%20has%20more%20features%2E%20As%20a%20general%20rule%2C%20you%20should%20use%20less%20instead%20of%20more%2E) [[Learn Linux]]
In `less`:
- Press the spacebar to move down one page
- Press `b` to move up one page
- Press `/` to search
- Press `q` to quit back to your shell prompt
